The story

Long ago, in the very beginning, God spoke light into the dark. A Light to Glory follows Pip, a brave (and slightly sarcastic) little lamb, and Lumi, a wise firefly, on a journey through the great stories of the Bible. From the garden in Genesis to a quiet stable in Bethlehem, every step leads closer to the light of Christ.

New episodes every Friday on YouTube, starting Christmas Day.
